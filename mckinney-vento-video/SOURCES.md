Each claim in the narration (`video/narration.json`) is listed by scene, with its source.

## Law and oversight (scene: law)
- McKinney-Vento Act, Education for Homeless Children and Youths (42 U.S.C. §§ 11431–11435): https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title42/chapter119/subchapter6/partB&edition=prelim
- National Center for Homeless Education (NCHE), McKinney-Vento legislation overview: https://nche.ed.gov/legislation/mckinney-vento/
- MSDE, McKinney-Vento program page (state oversight): https://www.marylandpublicschools.org/about/Pages/dsfss/homeless/index.aspx

## History (scene: history)
- 1987, Stewart B. McKinney Homeless Assistance Act, P.L. 100-77, signed July 22, 1987. Subtitle VII-B created the Education for Homeless Children and Youth program: https://www.congress.gov/bill/100th-congress/house-bill/558
  - Pennsylvania Dept. of Education summary: https://www.education.pa.gov/K-12/Homeless%20Education/mckinneyvento/Pages/default.aspx
- 2000, renamed McKinney-Vento, P.L. 106-400 (H.R. 5417), Oct. 30, 2000:
  - Statute: https://www.govinfo.gov/link/plaw/106/public/400
  - President Clinton's signing statement honoring Rep. Bruce Vento: https://clintonwhitehouse4.archives.gov/textonly/library/hot_releases/October_30_2000_4.html
- 2001, No Child Left Behind Act, P.L. 107-110, Title X Part C (liaison in every district, immediate enrollment, transportation to school of origin): https://www.congress.gov/bill/107th-congress/house-bill/1
- 2015, Every Student Succeeds Act, P.L. 114-95, signed Dec. 10, 2015, Title IX Part A: https://www.congress.gov/bill/114th-congress/senate-bill/1177
  - UNC School of Government summary of the law's history: https://www.sog.unc.edu/publications/bulletins/educational-rights-homeless-children-and-youths-mckinney-vento-act-and-its-impact-north-carolina%E2%80%99s

## Who is covered (scene: who)
- Definition of "homeless children and youths": doubled up, motels, shelters, cars, places not meant for habitation, and unaccompanied youth (42 U.S.C. § 11434a(2), (6)): https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11434a&num=0&edition=prelim
- Most identified students are doubled up, per NCHE federal data summaries: https://nche.ed.gov/data-and-stats/

## Student rights (scene: rights)
- Immediate enrollment without records, school of origin and best interest, transportation, dispute resolution with enrollment while it is pending (42 U.S.C. § 11432(g)(3)): https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11432&num=0&edition=prelim
- Automatic free school meals (categorical eligibility, 42 U.S.C. § 1758(b)(12)): https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1758&num=0&edition=prelim
- MSDE McKinney-Vento Fact Sheet (2023): https://www.marylandpublicschools.org/about/Documents/DSFSS/Homeless/McKinneyVentoFactsheet2023.pdf
- City Schools Board Policy JFE, Students Experiencing Homelessness: https://go.boarddocs.com/mabe/bcpss/Board.nsf/goto?open=&id=96DRWG6F891E
