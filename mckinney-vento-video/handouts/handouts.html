✂ Activity 6 · The Right Words: one set per group of three. Rotate roles for each scenario.
ARole-play
A usually on-time 4th grader has been late all week, falling asleep in class, and wearing the same clothes.
BRole-play
A 10th grader mentions they've been "couch surfing" and asks you not to tell anyone.
CRole-play
A parent at pickup says they're "between places right now" and worried about their child changing schools.

Observer checklist

  • Spoke privately, not in front of others
  • Used neutral words ("Where are you staying right now?") rather than "Are you homeless?"
  • Didn't promise secrecy they can't keep, but explained that information is shared only with people who can help
  • Named the next step: the school's McKinney-Vento liaison
  • Didn't make the student or family prove anything

Activity 1 · Agree / Disagree statements

StatementAnswer
1. A student living with their aunt's family because they were evicted counts as homeless under federal law.Agree: doubled up because of lost housing
2. A school can wait to enroll a student until the family brings proof of address.Disagree: enrollment is immediate, even without records
3. A student who moves to a shelter across the city has to change schools.Disagree: can stay in school of origin when in their best interest, with transportation
4. Teachers should tell the class when a student is homeless, so classmates can help.Disagree: housing status is confidential
5. A 16-year-old living on their own, without a parent, can enroll themselves in school.Agree: unaccompanied youth are covered and enrolled right away

Answer Key: Who Else Needs to Know?

CaseDepartmentsFirst step and key points
1McKinney-Vento liaison, special education / IEP team, transportationEnroll immediately and provide services comparable to the existing IEP right away; the liaison and IEP team coordinate records and transportation to the school of origin if that's in the student's best interest.
2McKinney-Vento liaison, ESOL office, registrar, nurseEnroll immediately without records; interpreter for the father; ESOL screening; the liaison helps obtain records and immunizations.
3McKinney-Vento liaison, counselor, college and career / financial aidThe liaison can verify Destiny is an unaccompanied homeless youth, so she can apply for financial aid as an independent student without her parent's information.
4Child Protective Services (mandated report), McKinney-Vento liaison, nurse, food services, counselor / social workerThe disclosure of harm requires a mandated report today. Living in a car is not, by itself, neglect; the report is about the abuse. Meet basic needs: meals, dental referral, and the liaison connects the family to shelter and services.
5McKinney-Vento liaison, early childhood / Pre-K, transportation, attendanceSchool of origin with transportation if in each child's best interest; restore the pre-K placement (preschoolers are covered); attendance supports, not penalties.
6Counselor / social worker, school psychologist / behavioral health, McKinney-Vento liaison"Doesn't see the point" may signal suicide risk: follow the school's crisis protocol the same day, then ongoing support and removal of academic barriers.
7McKinney-Vento liaison, registrar / counselor, athleticsProcedures must exist to award partial credit for work done at the previous school; work with athletics to remove barriers to staying on the team; transportation to school of origin if in his best interest.
8Registrar / front office, McKinney-Vento liaison, nurse, food servicesEnroll the same day without immunization records; liaison and nurse help get records; free meals start right away. Losing housing is not a reason for a report; keep the situation confidential.
